SoYiNote (the “App”) is developed and operated by Wang Yunzhao (“we” or “us”). This policy explains how the App handles your information.

Summary

The App does not collect or upload any of your personal information. The notes, lists, and settings you create are stored only on your device. We cannot see them, and we have no way to access them.

The App has no accounts and no servers of our own, and it does not include any third-party SDKs (including analytics, advertising, crash reporting, and push notifications).

Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect or upload any of the following information, and does not share it with any third party:

  • The notes, lists, titles, categories, dates, and footer text you write
  • Identity and contact information, such as your name, phone number, email address, and contacts
  • Data on your device, such as your location, the photos in your photo library, and the content of your reminders
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, and IP addresses
  • Usage records, activity logs, crash logs, and analytics data
  • Payment information, such as card numbers, your Apple Account password, and your billing address

Where Your Data Is Stored

Everything you create is stored only in the App’s storage on this device. It is not synced to the cloud or to other devices.

The App also stores a few settings on your device, such as Dark Mode, your theme, and a local record of your membership status (used to show your membership status when the App Store temporarily cannot be reached). This information also never leaves your device.

If you have turned on iCloud Backup or backups to a computer on your device, the system may include the App’s data in your backups under Apple’s rules. You control this in your device settings, and we cannot access these backups.

System Permissions

The App asks for a permission only when you tap the feature that needs it, never at launch:

  • Photos (Add Photos Only): When you tap Save to Photos, the App saves the long image or Lock Screen wallpaper it generated to your photo library. The App does not read the photos in your library.
  • Reminders: When you choose to load your reminders in Import, the App reads list names, reminder text, and completion status so that you can choose which ones to copy as lists. What it reads is used only on this device, and the App never changes your reminders in the Reminders app.

You can turn off these permissions at any time in your device’s Settings app. The App’s other features keep working as usual.

Membership Purchases

Purchases, charges, renewals, cancellations, and refunds of memberships are all handled by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your card information, Apple Account password, or billing address. The App only reads, on your device, the purchase records that Apple provides, to determine whether to enable membership features.

When prices and membership status are checked, the system’s App Store service communicates with Apple, and Apple handles that information under its own privacy policy.

Feedback

In Settings › Feedback, the text you write, together with the App’s version number and your iOS version, is passed to the system share sheet, and you choose which app to use and whom to send it to. The App never sends anything automatically.

How You Can Manage Your Information

All of your data is on your device, so you can directly:

  • View and edit: Open any note or list in the App.
  • Delete: After you take a note down, put it in the shredder in Taken Down to delete it permanently. Deleting the App removes all of its content from your device. Deleted content cannot be recovered, and we do not have a copy.
  • Withdraw permissions: Turn off the Photos or Reminders permission in your device’s Settings app.
  • Export: Save or send your content as a long image or Lock Screen wallpaper, or with the share feature.

Minors

The App is not designed for children, and it does not collect anyone’s personal information. Minors should have the consent of a parent or guardian before purchasing a membership.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date on this page and show the new version on the App’s Privacy Policy page. If we ever need to collect any information in the future, we will tell you separately and ask for your consent before collecting it.
