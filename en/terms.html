1. Member Benefits

The free version lets you:

  • Have up to 3 notes or lists hanging on the wall at a time (taken-down notes and lists do not count)
  • Use the default theme (Classic Wood) on the wall, and preview the other wall themes on the Theme page
  • Create Lock Screen wallpapers and long images with the default theme (Warm Wood); saved and shared images include the “SoYiNote” watermark
  • Read, edit, and take down all of your existing notes

With a membership, you get:

  • Unlimited notes and lists
  • All wall themes (board, frame, pins, and string)
  • All themes when creating Lock Screen wallpapers and long images
  • The option to turn off the “SoYiNote” watermark on shared images in Settings

Membership does not include cloud sync, multi-device sync, or account services. Your notes are stored only on the current device. Your membership is tied to the Apple Account used to purchase it: sign in with the same Apple Account on another device and tap Restore Purchases to use membership features there, but your notes will not be synced to that device.

2. Membership Plans and Prices

We offer the following plans. The price that applies is the amount shown on the membership page and in Apple’s payment confirmation, which the App Store provides for your region:

  • Monthly Membership: renews automatically every month. Eligible users can get a 7-day free trial with their first subscription.
  • Quarterly Membership: renews automatically every 3 months.
  • Yearly Membership: renews automatically every year.
  • Lifetime Membership: a one-time purchase that does not renew.

3. Auto-Renewal

Monthly, Quarterly, and Yearly Memberships are auto-renewing subscriptions. When you first purchase, Apple charges the fee for the first period right away (if your purchase includes a free trial, you are not charged during the trial). After that, Apple charges your Apple Account for the next period within 24 hours before the end of each period. Once the charge goes through, your membership automatically extends by one period, and this repeats until you cancel.

Free trial: You are not charged during the trial. Within 24 hours before the trial ends, Apple automatically charges the Monthly Membership price, and your first paid period begins. Each Apple Account can get only one free trial within the same subscription group.

Renewal reminder: When your subscription is set to renew automatically within 5 days, you will see a reminder once when you open the App, showing the renewal date, the amount, and how to cancel. If you do not open the App during that time, you will not see this reminder, so please watch for notifications from Apple and check your subscription settings.

Price changes: If the subscription price goes up, Apple will notify you in advance under its rules and ask for your consent where required.

4. How to Cancel

You can cancel auto-renewal at any time: on your iPhone, go to Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions, choose SoYiNote, and cancel; or in the App, go to Settings › Membership › Manage Subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period (or trial); otherwise, you may already have been charged for the next period. After you cancel, you can keep using membership features until the end of the period you have paid for, and you will not be charged again after it ends.

When you switch between Monthly, Quarterly, and Yearly, the new plan takes effect at the end of the current period (that is, on your next renewal date). If auto-renewal is turned off, switching plans turns it back on.

5. Lifetime Membership

Lifetime Membership is a one-time purchase and does not renew. “Lifetime” means that, for as long as the App continues to be offered, the Apple Account used for the purchase can keep using all of the membership features the App offered at the time of purchase. It is not a guarantee that any device or service will last permanently.

If you have an active subscription, buying Lifetime Membership does not cancel it automatically. This also applies when a subscription payment has not gone through and Apple is still retrying the charge. After your purchase, go to Manage Subscription and cancel the subscription yourself to avoid further charges. Until the subscription is canceled, the App’s membership page will keep showing its renewal status, and you will still get a reminder when it is set to renew within 5 days.

6. Refunds

Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple. To request a refund, use Apple’s Report a Problem page or contact Apple Support, and Apple will handle your request under its policies. If your refund is approved, access to the related membership features will be removed.

7. After Your Membership Ends or Is Refunded

After your membership expires, is canceled, or is refunded, all of the notes and lists you have written are kept. You can still read, edit, and take them down, and they will not be deleted or hidden. Only the following actions are subject to the free-version limits again: creating, importing, putting back from Taken Down, or undoing a take-down when it would bring the notes and lists on the wall to more than 3; creating Lock Screen wallpapers and long images with any theme other than the default theme; using any theme other than the default theme on the wall; and turning off the “SoYiNote” watermark when sharing. As a result, the wall returns to the default theme (Classic Wood), and the images you create include the “SoYiNote” watermark again. The wall theme and watermark settings you chose are kept, and they come back when you become a member again.

8. Changes to and Termination of the Service

We will keep maintaining the App. If we need to stop offering the App or the membership service for business or other reasons, we will let you know in the App in advance. After that, the installed App and your notes will remain on your device. For any subscription that is still active when the service stops, you can ask Apple to handle the fee for the unused portion.

9. Minors

Users under 18 should purchase a membership only with the consent of a parent or guardian. A parent or guardian can restrict in-app purchases with Screen Time on iPhone, or approve purchases with Ask to Buy in Family Sharing.

Matters not covered by this agreement are governed by the Apple Media Services Terms and Conditions and the App’s Terms of Use.
