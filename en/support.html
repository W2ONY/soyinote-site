Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings › Membership, tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the page, and use the Apple Account you made the purchase with. You can restore your membership this way on a new device, or after deleting and reinstalling the App.

How do I cancel auto-renewal?

On your iPhone, go to Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions, choose SoYiNote, and cancel; or in the App, go to Settings › Membership › Manage Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period or trial.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple. Request a refund through Apple’s Report a Problem page or Apple Support.

Will I lose my notes when my membership ends?

No. All of your existing notes and lists are kept, and you can still read, edit, and take them down. The only changes: you can’t create, import, put back, or undo a take-down when it would bring the notes and lists on the wall to more than 3; Lock Screen wallpapers and long images can use only the default theme (Warm Wood) and include the “SoYiNote” watermark; and the wall returns to the default theme (Classic Wood). The wall theme you chose is kept, and it comes back when you become a member again.

Will my notes still be there after I switch phones?

The App has no cloud sync, and your notes are stored only on your device. When you switch phones, you can transfer your data with Quick Start on iPhone or restore from an iCloud Backup; the system usually brings app data along, subject to Apple’s transfer rules. To get your membership on the new device, just tap Restore Purchases.

How do I delete my data?

After you take a note down, put it in the shredder in Taken Down to delete it permanently. Deleting the App removes all of its content from your device, and it cannot be recovered. The App does not collect any personal information, and we do not have a copy of your data.
